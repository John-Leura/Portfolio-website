About Me

Hello, I’m John Leura, a professional currently working at Offutt Air Force Base with an academic background in computer science and cybersecurity.

My coursework has given me hands-on experience across application development, SQL databases, cloud deployment, steganography, web technologies, and game development. I bring curiosity, a practical approach to learning, and a commitment to continuing to develop my technical skills in my professional work.
